SCENE 01 / A PLACE TO BEGIN

What if a room could tell a story?

The experience

Users enter a small scene and discover its meaning through proximity, visual rhythm, ambient sound, and objects that respond to attention.

Design direction

Keep the interaction vocabulary small: move, look, listen, and select. Give the environment enough silence for the user to notice details and form their own interpretation.

Next step

Prototype one room in Blender or Three.js, define one interaction loop, and document how spatial decisions guide exploration without forcing a linear path.
