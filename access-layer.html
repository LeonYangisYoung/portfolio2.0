Designing for more ways of using.

The challenge

Many interfaces assume that everyone reads, sees, moves, and understands information in the same way. Access Layer explores how a product can make its core controls clearer and more adaptable.

Design direction

Clear hierarchy, strong contrast, larger targets, plain-language labels, keyboard-friendly flows, and settings that change the experience without hiding essential content.

Next step

Build the prototype, then test the flow with people who use different access needs. This concept currently contains no fabricated user research or test results.
